What we heard

The brief

A desktop canvas where references, notes, images, PDFs and sketches are pinned, linked and arranged. The mark should feel crafty, calm and personal, stay clear at 16 px, and avoid the sticky-note and bright-multicolour clichés of canvas tools.

craftycalmpersonalorganised mess

Success: reads as one shape at 16 px, works in one colour, owns the app's muted palette, distinct from overlapping-card icons.

Log Cabin

Four patches turn around one odd piece: a quilt block that is also a canvas of cards.

  • One pinwheel shape that holds up at 16 px and as an app tile
  • Each patch takes one of the app's own colours; the diamond is you
  • Built on one grid: 12-unit gaps, 12-unit radii, a 45° centre piece
